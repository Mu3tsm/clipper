Clipper

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Clipper is a personal desktop tool. It turns short gaming moments into vertical clips and uploads them to the owner's own TikTok and YouTube accounts. It is used only by its owner and is not offered to the public.

Terms of Service

Clipper is a private tool for the owner's own accounts. Nobody else may use it. It is provided as is, without any warranty. You must follow the TikTok and YouTube terms of service and community guidelines whenever you use it, and you need permission from the creators whose content appears in your clips.

Privacy Policy

Clipper runs on the owner's own computer. When you log in, TikTok and Google send it access tokens. It keeps them only on that computer and uses them for one thing: uploading the owner's clips to the owner's accounts. Clipper does not collect, sell, or share personal data, and it does not send data to anyone except TikTok and Google, through their official APIs. To revoke access at any time, open your TikTok or Google account settings.
